    What you need to know about Phuket?

    Phuket is the largest island in Thailand spread over 222 sq mi. Owing to its appearance the name of the island was derived from the word bukitin Malay which means hill. In the Patong beach, as early as the 1970’s beach bungalows started attracting tourists. This marked the beginning of the tourism on the island and there has been no turning back. The island that was once known as a popular supplier for tin and rubber is one of the largest tourism hubs in Thailand, today. Whether it is to set sail on a yacht over the weekend or to have a lazy beach vacation, this island has a lot to offer for the tourist. How is the island as a place of residence? Imagine living on a beach holiday villa, every day! Besides the blue beaches and the hot and humid weather, there is so much more to do on this beautiful island.

    Where do foreigners live in Phuket?

    Phuket, renowned for its stunning landscapes and tranquil lifestyle, is a preferred destination for foreigners who wish to experience the best of what the tropical island has to offer. With a diverse range of neighborhoods and living options, Phuket ensures that foreign residents can find the perfect location to call their new home. A few prime areas where foreigners tend to reside in Phuket include:

    1. Patong: As the heart of Phuket's nightlife scene, Patong is a bustling area that attracts a considerable number of foreign residents. With an abundance of shopping, dining, and recreational facilities, Patong is an ideal location for those who desire a lively environment.

    2. Kata and Karon: Located towards the south of Patong, Kata and Karon are serene and picturesque coastal towns that appeal to families and retirees. These areas offer a variety of housing options, from affordable apartments to luxurious villas, and with easy access to pristine beaches and essential amenities, these towns have become increasingly popular among foreign residents.

    3. Kamala: Situated along the west coast, Kamala is a delightful beachside town that provides a laid-back atmosphere for foreigners who value tranquility and privacy. With a range of high-end residential properties, Kamala is a favorite among affluent expats and long-term visitors.

    4. Surin and Bang Tao: These neighboring areas boast stunning beaches, luxury resorts, and prestigious gated communities, making them attractive to foreigners seeking an upscale living experience. Surin and Bang Tao offer a wide array of amenities and leisure activities, and are well-regarded for their elite reputations and first-class facilities.

    5. Phuket Town: As the island's administrative and cultural center, Phuket Town is home to a diverse mix of foreign residents. The town provides a unique living experience, featuring charming Sino-Portuguese architecture and a sophisticated art scene. Phuket Town caters to foreign residents seeking a blend of modern conveniences and traditional Thai heritage.
